Meteors - Friday April 10th at 7:30pm

From the Composer

Meteors follows the trajectory of an asteroid through solar winds, the asteroid belt, and ultimate impact — not as a story of destruction, but of childlike wonder at the sheer scale of the universe. At the moment of collision, time fractures and the orchestra grinds to a halt; until a sweeping melody emerges, leaving only awe: a curtain opening onto a world newly revealed.

A Larger Vision

This performance is the first glimpse of an evening-length multimedia trilogy combining orchestral music with dance, film, and theater — exploring origins, creation myths, and our increasingly complicated relationship with the natural world.

To follow the project, find @yuvalcomposer on Instagram — or reach out at music@yuvalmedina.com to get involved.

The Composer

Yuval Medina is a composer and pianist working at the intersection of music with dance, film, and the concert stage. His orchestral rescore of BBC's Frozen Planet II has been viewed over 2.8 million times on YouTube.
